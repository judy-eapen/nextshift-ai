What it does

  • 🔭
    Explore 1,017 real careers instantly — search plain words, browse 16 families, 7 “what you like doing” traits, 18 school subjects
  • ✨
    Surfaces careers students never hear about, fast-growing and declining ones, and the difference between them
  • 🧾
    Career pages answer 15 questions; every figure names its source, year and retrieval date
  • 🤖
    Reviewed AI summaries on request — generated once, cached for everyone
  • 💬
    Adaptive interview for students; guided intake for professionals; saved careers become quoted evidence, never a choice
  • 📋
    Evidence-cited 30-day / 6-month / 1-year preparation plan, approved by the person before it is saved

Key outcomes

  • 0 model calls while browsing, searching or comparing
  • Every factual line cites an evidence card [cNN]
  • A different model family reviews every claim
  • Missing data says “not available” — never estimated
  • Jobs with no official category become labelled composites
  • Nothing written without explicit human approval

How it works (high level)

  1. You browse a catalog built from official data — instantly, no AI involved
  2. Every figure carries its source and year; gaps say “data not available”
  3. You save careers and react; comparing shows the same rows for each, never a score
  4. On request, a model summarizes a page — from its sourced facts only
  5. Fixed checks and a second model strip anything unsupported
  6. The interview uses what you saved as things you noticed, not chose
  7. Evidence is gathered in parallel and disagreements are shown side by side
  8. You approve before anything is saved; next time it shows what changed

Data & trust

  • Evidence-first: every claim traces to a card with source + date
  • Outlook (BLS) kept separate from AI task use (observed, not a forecast) — a career can be both AI-heavy and growing
  • No “AI-proof”, no safety scores, no personality labels — linted out
  • Confidence, unknowns and disagreement shown, never averaged
  • Privacy: local files, no accounts, tracing off outside developer mode

Deliberately hard cases

  • “Product Manager” — no official category → composite, human ticks the tasks

  • Reviewer outage → UNVERIFIED, never silent
  • Declining career → openings still shown (“fewer jobs ≠ no way in”)
